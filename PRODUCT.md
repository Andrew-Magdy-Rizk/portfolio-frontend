# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Hiring managers, collaborators, and prospective clients evaluating Andrew Magdy for web-development work. They need a quick, credible sense of his craft, technical focus, and how to start a conversation.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio that introduces Andrew Magdy and presents his web-development practice. Success means a visitor can understand his focus and confidently reach out from any device.

## Positioning

Andrew Magdy combines full-stack engineering with thoughtful web experiences; the portfolio itself demonstrates that attention to craft.

## Operating Context

Visitors often arrive from a job application, referral, social profile, or shared link and typically view the site on a phone before exploring it on a larger screen.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The project is a responsive Next.js web portfolio. It must remain usable on small touch screens and respect reduced-motion preferences. Project examples, contact details, and portrait assets have not yet been supplied and must not be fabricated as factual claims.

## Brand Commitments

Personal brand portfolio. The supplied reference is binding as an inspiration for its immersive, type-led dark composition, while the resulting experience should be calmer and more restrained.

## Evidence on Hand

The repository contains an early Next.js portfolio shell and no verified project case studies, contact links, or personal portrait asset.

## Product Principles

- Make the developer's character clear in the first viewport.
- Let visual craft demonstrate technical care rather than relying on inflated claims.
- Give visitors a direct, low-friction path to make contact.
- Treat the mobile experience as a first-class introduction, not a compressed desktop page.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Responsive mobile-first layout, keyboard-accessible controls, clear contrast, visible focus states, and reduced-motion support are required.
